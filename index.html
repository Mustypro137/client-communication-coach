Three ways to get better at client work

Practise with a client

Seven realistic situations: a new inquiry, a discount request, a late payment, a complaint and more. Reply as you would in a real chat.

Draft your replies

Paste a real client message, choose a goal and a tone, and get an editable draft. Check your English before you send it yourself.

Track your progress

See your scores, your daily streak and your skills grow: tone, good questions, clear English and tricky moments.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a scenario

    Start with a beginner one, like a new website inquiry.

  2. 2

    Reply to the client

    Write your answer in English, the way you would on WhatsApp.

  3. 3

    Read your feedback and try again

    Your coach shows what went well, what to fix, and an improved reply.

Good to know

  • Template-based, not real AI. The clients are scripted and the feedback comes from simple, transparent rules. It doesn't understand every possible reply.
  • Your data stays in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking. Browser storage is not encrypted, so don't paste anything secret.
  • Nothing is sent to your clients. You copy a reply and send it yourself.
